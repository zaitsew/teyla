Teyla is the inbox for a one-person software studio. It shows what waits on you, where every product stands, and what got done while you were away.

Today
- One sentence on what matters now, then the questions only you can answer, ranked by what they unblock.
- Answer by text or by voice. Voice is turned into text on your Mac; nothing is sent until you press Send.
- The tasks only you can do: a pull request to merge, an issue assigned to you.
- What finished since yesterday, and what runs now.
- Questions that have waited more than a week fold into one "Older" section, so fresh ones stay on top.

Portfolio
- Every product with a health word: off track, at risk, on track or parked, and the reason in one line.
- Each platform on its own line: iOS, macOS, Android, web. Version, build and state, such as "current" or "behind".
- Open pull requests, pull requests that wait on you, recent activity and the next step.

How it works
Teyla for Mac from the App Store is a sandboxed companion. It reads the same signed folder in your own iCloud Drive that the Teyla iPhone app reads, and leaves signed answers there. There is no Teyla server and no account, and the app collects no data. Not connected yet? Choose "See a sample" to try every screen with made-up data.

The full app
The App Store version cannot run commands. The full Teyla for Mac runs the local teyla command and your coding tools. It installs from GitHub with install.sh. Both are free and open source.
