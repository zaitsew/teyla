Privacy

Teyla reads your session logs, your repos and your config on your Mac. It has no server, no account, no telemetry and no analytics SDK. It never uploads a transcript. Nothing about your sessions leaves your Mac unless you turn on a feature that says so.

What leaves your Mac, and when

WhatWhenSends
Update checkOnce a day, on by defaultThe version number, to GitHub
teyla spend, teyla reviews, teyla stateWhen you run themRepo names, PR numbers and your login, through your own gh
teyla answer, teyla run, --model flagsOnly when you askText, to your model provider under your own login
teyla relay onOff by defaultOpen questions and session titles, to your iCloud Drive

Safe mode, teyla config set safe.enabled=true, closes most of these calls. The full list names every call and its host.

The Mac and iPhone apps

The apps collect nothing and have no account. The iPhone app reads a signed folder in your own iCloud Drive. Apple holds the keys to that folder unless you turn on Advanced Data Protection. RELAY.md lists what the folder holds.

The Claude Code plugin

The plugin uploads nothing. Its hook scripts and skills contain no network call. The plugin README lists what it reads and writes on your machine.

This site

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from another host. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles each request as any web host does.
