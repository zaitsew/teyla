# Log

Append-only. One line per change to this wiki: date, what changed, source,
who confirmed. Never edit or delete a line — a wrong entry gets a follow-up
line, not a correction in place. This is the audit trail a human reads to
trust a page they did not write.

Line format:

    - <YYYY-MM-DD> — <what changed> (source: <session/file/URL>, confirmed by: agent|human)
